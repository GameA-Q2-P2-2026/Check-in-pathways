Game Design weekly check-ins

Once a week, show your teacher what you finished, say what is next, and rate how it is going. Start with Unity Essentials, then check in on the path you choose.

  1. Show the work you finished this week.
  2. Say what you finished, what is next, and what is blocking you.
  3. Rate your week: green, yellow, or red.
  4. Commit to one goal for next week.
