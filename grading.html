How you are graded

Your grade is out of 100 points. Every point you earn counts toward your percentage.

Where the 100 points come from
CategoryPointsWhat it is

Path work30Your weekly progress on your path, 6 points a week.

Weekly check-ins (4 points each)

  • 2 points: every part is filled in by check-in day.
  • 1 point: you show your evidence, or honestly say what is not finished.
  • 1 point: your color matches your evidence and your goal is specific enough to finish in a week.

Your color is never graded. Reporting yellow or red honestly earns full credit, and it is how your teacher knows to help.

Path work (6 points a week)

ScoreWhat your evidence shows
6The week's target is met, and the work is clearly yours
5Target met, but the evidence is thin or you cannot explain it
4About three-quarters of the target
3About half of the target
2Started, with something real to show
1 or 0Minimal work, or none

If you cannot explain something you turned in, the week scores 5 or lower until you can. Be ready to explain your own work.

Group project (30 points)

  • Team product, 12 points: the game runs, the parts fit together, and it is finished and tested. Your whole team gets the same score.
  • Your contribution, 12 points: you deliver what your role promised, the work is good, and you work steadily for both weeks.
  • Teamwork, 6 points: teammates rate you as reliable, you communicate, and you help others.

Reflection and showcase (10 points)

Answer five reflection prompts with specific examples (5 points), then show the game running, explain your role, name a problem you solved, answer a question, and finish on time (5 points).
