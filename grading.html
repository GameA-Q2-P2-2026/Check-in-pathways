How you are graded

The course is worth 1000 points. Unity Essentials and your path add up to 800 points, and the final group project is worth 200. Your percentage is your points divided by 1000.

Essentials100
Check-ins200
Path work500
Final project200
800 points: Essentials and your path 200 points

Unity Essentials

100 points

  • Pathway completion, 60 points: the share of Unity Essentials you finish.
  • Checkpoint scene, 40 points: a simple scene that runs, has lighting, and has one interactive element, and you can explain your choices.

Weekly check-ins

200 points

Seven check-ins: two in Essentials and five on your path. Each path check-in is worth 32 points and each Essentials check-in is worth 20.

What countsPathEssentials
Every part is filled in by check-in day1610
You show your evidence, or honestly say what is not finished85
Your color matches your evidence, and your goal is specific enough to finish in a week85

Your color is never graded. Reporting yellow or red honestly earns full credit, and it is how your teacher knows to help.

If you cannot explain something you turned in, the week scores 85 or lower until you can. Be ready to explain your own work.

Final group project

200 points

  • Team product, 60 points: the game runs, the parts fit together, and it is finished and tested. Your whole team gets the same score.
  • Your contribution, 60 points: you deliver what your role promised, the work is good, and you work steadily for both weeks.
  • Teamwork, 30 points: teammates rate you as reliable, you communicate, and you help others.
  • Written reflection, 25 points: five prompts, answered with specific examples.
  • Showcase demo, 25 points: show the game running, explain your role, name a problem you solved, answer a question, and finish on time.
